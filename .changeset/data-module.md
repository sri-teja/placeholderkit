New `@placeholderkit/core/data` module: `createWorld()` returns a complete, internally consistent fake business for prototypes, with images already attached. Ecommerce worlds have customers, products, orders and reviews across eight store types; SaaS worlds have accounts, users, plans, subscriptions, invoices, an activity feed and support tickets. Includes en-US, en-GB and en-IN locales, monthly metrics for charts, `toJSON`/`toCSV`/`toSQL` exports (Postgres, MySQL, SQLite) and a CLI: `npx @placeholderkit/core data`.
